# RoleAide demo production plan

Prepared October 27, 2026. Production is gated on verification of the deployed upload-to-ranked-jobs flow. This file is a script/shot plan, not a finished video or a claim of live acceptance.

## Deliverables

- A 60–75 second landscape walkthrough for LinkedIn, portfolio and applications.
- A separately framed 25–35 second vertical cut for WhatsApp status, Instagram and X.
- Narration, accurate captions, thumbnail and a short post linking to the verified product.

Use actual screen recordings of the app. Use a clearly fictional CV with no real contact information. Record the deployed version once verified; show its release/date in the production log. Do not display environment files, auth codes, tokens, inboxes, private account data or confidential benchmark work. A localhost/staging or mocked-service demonstration must be labelled as such.

## Landscape script and shot list

| Time | Shot | Narration draft |

| 0–7s | Homepage primary upload action; product wordmark | “A CV tells your story. Finding which jobs to explore can still take hours.” |

Only retain lines supported by the release actually recorded. Hide waiting time with clearly editorial cuts; do not fabricate successful requests or substitute fixture jobs without disclosure. The wordmark and brand-mark.svg in public/ can be used as existing assets; no generated replacement UI is necessary.

## Low-cost workflow

1. Verify the complete deployed flow and decide the one user problem to demonstrate.
2. Record at 1440×900 or 1920×1080 with consistent browser zoom. Capture clean takes per shot, leave a moment before/after actions, and keep important text readable on phones.
3. Use a brief narration recorded by Victor, or an explicitly selected licensed synthetic voice. No voice cloning required. Keep music optional and use licensed assets.
4. Edit cuts, cursor emphasis, limited zooms and captions; keep job-source attribution visible. Export H.264 MP4 at 1080p with AAC audio and provide an SRT caption file.
5. Make the vertical cut by reframing useful UI regions; do not merely shrink a desktop screen into an unreadable phone frame.
6. Review the final video against the product and narration, then prepare platform-specific copy. Publication requires the user's instruction; nothing is auto-posted.

## Available capabilities and current limits

Playwright is installed for screenshots, browser-driven recording and reproducible captures. No system ffmpeg or speech synthesizer was found in the initial command check. No recording, TTS generation or paid video service call has been made.

The plugin directory lists Descript for conversational editing, captions, audio cleanup and video creation. It was suggested but installation/connection is unconfirmed. Other listed tools include Runway and HeyGen for generated video/voiceover. Their presence in a directory is not account access or a free entitlement. Prefer real product footage and existing local tools before paying for synthetic marketing visuals. Confirm actual credits and prices before any service spends money.
